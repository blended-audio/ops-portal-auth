Privacy Policy

BA OPS PORTAL Discord application. Last updated: 17 August 2026.

This policy explains what data the BA OPS PORTAL Discord application collects, how it is used, who it is shared with, and how to request its deletion.

The application is an internal operational tool used solely by staff and contracted freelancers of Blended Audio. It is not offered to the public and cannot be added to servers other than our own.

Who is responsible

Blended Audio, South Africa. Contact for all privacy queries and deletion requests: stefan.vosloo@blendedaudio.co.za.

What data we collect

  • Discord account identifiers. Your Discord user ID, username and display name.
  • Direct message content. The content of messages you exchange directly with the application.
  • Nominated channel message content. The content of messages posted in the specific server channels an administrator has configured the application to read. Channels are opted in individually. Channels that have not been configured are not read.
  • Interaction data. When you click a button or submit a form presented by the application, we record which action you took, when, and any text you entered.
  • Operational records. Work allocations, deadlines, acknowledgements, issue reports and related job information associated with you as part of your work with us.

We do not collect message content from private channels the application has not been configured to read, from other servers, or from direct messages between you and anyone other than the application.

Why we collect it, and the legal basis

The data is used solely to operate our internal production workflow: sending you work allocations and deadline notices, recording that you received and acknowledged them, capturing issues you raise, and maintaining an accurate operational record of who was informed of what and when.

Our lawful basis is the performance of a contract with you, and our legitimate interest in maintaining accurate operational and accountability records.

Where it is stored and who processes it

  • Google Workspace (Google Sheets and Google Apps Script) is where operational records are stored and processed.
  • Cloudflare Workers relays messages between our systems and Discord. Message content passes through Cloudflare in transit. It is not retained there.
  • Discord operates the messaging platform itself and is governed by its own privacy policy at discord.com/privacy.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your data and we do not share it for advertising or marketing. Access is limited to our management and the administrators of our internal operations portal. Beyond the service providers listed above, data is disclosed to third parties only where required by law.

How long we keep it

Operational records, including acknowledgements and issue reports, are retained for as long as we require them for accountability, payment and dispute-resolution purposes, and thereafter in line with our record-keeping obligations. Discord account identifiers are retained for as long as your account is linked to our portal.

Your rights

You may request access to the data we hold about you, correction of anything inaccurate, deletion of your data, or restriction of its processing. You may also withdraw consent to the Discord integration at any time and continue to use the operations portal directly instead.

To make any of these requests, contact stefan.vosloo@blendedaudio.co.za. We will respond within the period required by applicable law.

Unlinking your Discord account removes the link between your Discord identifier and your portal profile and stops all further messaging. Operational records that we are required to retain for accountability or payment purposes are kept, and are no longer associated with your Discord identifier.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page and notify affected users through the operations portal.

This application is used internally by a single organisation. It is not listed in the Discord App Directory and is not available for public installation.
